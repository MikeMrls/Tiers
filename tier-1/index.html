About the author

Adrian W. Mercer

Novelist, essayist, and cultural historian.

Adrian W. Mercer writes about memory, identity, and history, and about the quiet complexities of everyday life. His prose is reflective and his characters carefully observed, and his work sits where literary fiction meets cultural criticism and philosophy.

His books look at how personal histories shape collective memory, how cities hold on to the stories of the people who live in them, and how ordinary moments can reveal extraordinary truths. He blends historical detail with a contemporary eye, and he is happiest in old buildings, second-hand bookshops, and other people's attics.
