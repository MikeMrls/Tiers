About the author

Adrian W. Mercer

A British novelist and essayist who writes about memory, identity, and the quiet complexities of everyday life.

His books ask how personal histories shape collective memory, how cities hold the stories of the people who live in them, and how ordinary moments reveal extraordinary truths.
