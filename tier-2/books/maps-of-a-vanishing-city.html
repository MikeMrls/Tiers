Maps of a Vanishing City

Fourteen essays on the streets we lose, and what they take with them.

Buy on Amazon → All retailers

Synopsis

Over five years Adrian W. Mercer walked the same city again and again, notebook in hand, recording what disappeared: a bookshop, a cinema, a tram depot, the public baths where three generations learned to swim.

Maps of a Vanishing City collects fourteen essays on urban change and the quiet ways neighborhoods fade, and on what happens to our sense of self when the places that made us are gone.

Part history, part elegy, it is a field guide to the city we carry in our heads.
