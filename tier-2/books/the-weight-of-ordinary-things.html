The Weight of Ordinary Things

A teacup, a house key, a coat left on a hook. Mercer finds whole lives in the things we stop noticing.

Buy on Amazon → All retailers

Synopsis

In twenty-two short essays, Adrian W. Mercer turns his attention to the objects and rituals we stop seeing: the spare key, the Sunday roast, the coat that stays on its hook for a year after its owner has gone.

Moving between memoir, social history, and philosophy, he asks why some things grow heavier the longer we keep them, and what our kitchens, drawers, and mantelpieces remember on our behalf.

Tender, exact, and quietly funny, The Weight of Ordinary Things is a book about paying attention, and about the lives hidden in plain sight.
